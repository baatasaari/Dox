Risk & Mitigation

We Know the Risks.
Here's How We Address Them.

Big Tech Moves In

Medium

AWS, Azure, or Google ships a native agent governance product.

Mitigation: Cloud-agnostic is our moat. Customers with multi-cloud or on-prem agents can't use a single-cloud solution. Compliance buyers need vendor-neutral audit evidence. We also move faster — we shipped in months, not quarters.

Observability Players Expand

Medium

LangSmith or Arize adds enforcement capabilities.

Mitigation: Enforcement requires deep compliance and security domain expertise — not just engineering. Our policy corpus, regulatory mapping, and audit chain are 12+ months ahead. The governance buyer (CISO/Legal) is different from the observability buyer (DevOps).

Mitigation: Regulatory requirements (EU AI Act) create demand for governance regardless of adoption pace. Even a 50% reduction in agent adoption still leaves a $5B+ market. We're the fire extinguisher — demand goes up when things go wrong, not down.

Mitigation: Compliance mandates (EU AI Act fines, SEC scrutiny) compress cycles. 14-day POC gets real data into hands fast — first sentinel alert closes deals faster than any sales deck. We target champions with budget authority, not IT procurement.
